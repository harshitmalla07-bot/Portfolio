A team client-design project for McMaster's ENG 1P13. Our client uses a wheelchair and needed a tool to reach and grab objects. Over 13 weeks, I took three concept designs through a weighted decision matrix to a working 3D-printed prototype.

I modeled every component in Autodesk Inventor and iterated through several PLA print revisions. In load testing, the main rod held 48 N while the end effector gripped at 9.8 N. The first claw kept losing its grip in testing, so I redesigned its geometry around a 45° contact angle.

I presented the final design and test data to the client and a faculty panel.
